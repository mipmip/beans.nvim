1. INTRODUCTION                                             *beans-introduction*

beans.nvim opens a small wizard when the Beans TUI creates a bean in your
$EDITOR, letting you set frontmatter fields with single keystrokes.

See beans-nvim-briefing.md in the repository for the complete design spec.

==============================================================================
2. SETUP                                                          *beans-setup*

>lua
    require("beans").setup()
<

`setup()` with no arguments yields the intended experience.
